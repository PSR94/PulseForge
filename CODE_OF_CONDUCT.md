# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free open-source community. Be constructive, assume good intent while still reviewing rigorously, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people. Harassment, threats, discriminatory conduct, and disclosure of private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove contributions or participation that violate these expectations. Security reports and moderation concerns should be sent privately through the repository's supported contact channels.
